How it works

Is my data private?

Yes. All calculations run in your browser with JavaScript. No account, no tracking, no server — your numbers never leave this page. They are saved only in your own browser (localStorage) if you want them remembered.

What does the payment include?

Principal and interest only (P&I). It does not include property tax, homeowners insurance, PMI, or HOA fees. Add those separately when you budget the full monthly cost.

What assumptions does it make?

Fixed: a fixed rate, monthly payments, and extra payments applied directly to principal. ARM: the rate is fixed for the phase you enter, then index + margin, capped as above, with the payment re-calculated over the remaining term at each adjustment. The index is held constant — the real index will move. If your lender applies extra payments differently, check your loan statement.

Is this financial advice?

No — this is an educational calculator. Paying a mortgage early is a personal decision that depends on your other debts, emergency fund, and goals. Talk to a licensed professional for advice specific to you.
