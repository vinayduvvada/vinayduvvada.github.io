How it works

Is my data private?

Yes. Bills are saved only in your browser (localStorage). No account, no server, no tracking.

What counts as due soon?

Any unpaid bill due within the next 7 days. It moves back to Upcoming if you change the due date.

What happens when I mark a monthly bill paid?

It stays in Paid for this cycle, and a new Upcoming copy is created for next month with the due date advanced by one month.

Is this financial advice?

No — this is an educational organizer. It shows cash flow, it does not tell you what to pay or when.
