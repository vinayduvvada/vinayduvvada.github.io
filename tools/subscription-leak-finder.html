How it works

Is my statement private?

Yes. The CSV is parsed with JavaScript in your browser. It is never uploaded, stored on a server, or sent anywhere.

What counts as recurring?

The same merchant name (after cleaning up numbers and location codes) appearing at least twice, with amounts within a small range or with a clear price change.

What gets flagged?

A price increase between the first and last charge, and similar merchant names that may be the same service billed twice.

What CSV format works?

Most bank/card exports work: a header row with Date, Description, and Amount columns (in any order). Amounts can be negative for spending, or positive — the tool uses the absolute value.
